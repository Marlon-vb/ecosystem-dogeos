01 · PropAMM

The most capital-efficient way
to build liquidity onchain.

Capital required for tight execution
Capital needed to fill an order at roughly 10 bps of price impact, passive pool vs Keyrock PropAMM.
Passive pool TVLPropAMM capital
Illustrative figures. The mechanism: PropAMM capital recycles against hedges instead of sitting idle across the whole curve.

Roughly 10x more capital efficient. A passive pool spreads capital across every price; a PropAMM concentrates it where the market actually trades and re-centres continuously, so the same depth costs a fraction of the TVL.

And Keyrock is built to run it. A PropAMM is only as tight as its off-chain liquidity sourcing. With top-tier fee schedules on every major exchange, Keyrock recycles inventory without execution costs, which means tighter DOGE spreads than any DeFi-native operator can quote.

03 · Money markets

Idle DOGE becomes working capital.

04 · RWA vaults

Real-world yield, with instant liquidity.

Instant liquidity, in one transaction. A Keyrock-curated vault holds USDC and quotes fair RWA swaps, priced by the same off-chain market making engine that runs our books, and plugged into the main intent and routing platforms so holder flow finds it from any interface.

Capital never idles. When redemption demand is low, surplus deploys into money markets, and as a KYC'd institution Keyrock mints and redeems with the issuer at each window to keep the vaults balanced while volume ramps.

And the token stays composable. As a power user of DeFi, Keyrock drives utility for the RWA across the ecosystem: curated markets on Morpho, collateral listings on Aave, fixed-rate venues, plus the models and know-how to shape DogeOS's tokenisation strategy end to end.
