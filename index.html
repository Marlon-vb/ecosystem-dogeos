How it works: hover or tap a card to spotlight it in the live simulation.

01
Live fair value

Quotes are anchored to a real time feed across CEX and onchain venues, not to the pool's last trade.

02
Dynamic quoting

Spread widens with volatility and tightens in calm markets. Stale prices never sit on the curve waiting to be picked off.

03
Inventory management

Filled orders skew the next quotes, so the book mean-reverts rather than accumulating one sided risk.

04
Router flow capture

Aggregators route to the best price. Sharper quotes win retail flow for the DOGE pool instead of leaking it to arbitrage.
